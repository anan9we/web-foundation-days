About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for capturing ideas, reminders, and notes before they disappear. It helps you keep important thoughts organized and easy to access.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your idea or reminder into the new note field.
  2. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  3. Review your notes in the Your Notes section.

Features

  • Quick note capture
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface
  • Organized list of notes
  • Keyboard shortcuts for common actions

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
Ctrl + S Save your note
